⚡ How to Play

  • Tap a card to reveal its symbol.
  • Reveal another card to find its pair.
  • Matching cards stay open.
  • Remember the locations of symbols.
  • Find every pair to finish the game.

🎮 Difficulty Levels

  • Easy: 6 pairs (12 cards)
  • Medium: 8 pairs (16 cards)
  • Hard: 12 pairs (24 cards)

💡 Pro Tips

Look carefully at each revealed card. Remember both the symbol and its position to make fewer moves.

🏆 Scoring

Your score rewards matching pairs and efficient play. Finish quickly with fewer moves to aim for a higher score.
