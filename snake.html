⚡ GAME GUIDE

🎯 How to Play

  • Press Start Game to begin.
  • Guide the snake toward the glowing food.
  • Each food item gives you 10 points.
  • Your snake grows after eating.
  • Don't hit the wall or your own body!

🏆 Level System

Every 50 points increases your level and makes the snake move faster. Keep going and set a new personal best!

💡 Pro Tips

  • Make wide turns.
  • Look ahead before turning.
  • Avoid reversing directly into yourself.
  • Pause when you need a break.
